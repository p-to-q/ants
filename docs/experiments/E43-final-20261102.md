## Final decision

At the registered ≤300 parameter endpoint and 50,400 CPU-second budget, the 16 paired differences have median **0**; exact Wilcoxon p **0.5771484375**, above the fixed per-look threshold **0.0294**. The Hodges–Lehmann estimate is about -0.0002, with exact interval [-0.0065, +0.0008] (coverage 0.956). Arm medians are 0.8153 control and 0.8156 screen; these do not replace the paired statistic.

There is no evidence for adopting screening on the registered primary endpoint. This does not establish equivalence or bound every possible benefit. Including the first budget-crossing finished trial changes none of the 32 primary endpoints or the final paired statistics. Keep the registered script and both boundary interpretations in the record.

At ≤3,000 parameters the median paired difference is approximately -0.0024 and the reported exploratory Wilcoxon p is 0.0003. This was one of many secondary rows and is not a replacement primary endpoint or a confirmation. A targeted new protocol could examine whether screening specifically helps at that cap. No seeds 17+ are appended to E43.

Median s1 CPU shares are approximately 0.55 control / 0.50 screen; this remains below the protocol's 20% relative workload diagnostic threshold. The power-derived minimum detectable effect is a planning diagnostic, not a confidence bound.

## Preserving models and updating the public frontier

The local candidate store preserves four cap-specific specifications for each of 32 runs, with immutable checksummed versions and per-scope current-best pointers. Historical weights were not retained by the installed gym. New exports therefore explicitly say `refitted_train_only`: numeric weights, preprocessing, source, manifest and prediction parity, fitted using development training segments only. They do not reproduce historical validation checkpoints or acquire champion status.

The public README frontier is refreshed from all 32 ledgers. Nominal cap / best recorded parameter count / development rel_mse: 100 / 98 / 0.824718; 300 / 295 / 0.806931; 1,000 / 900 / 0.779906; 3,000 / 3,000 / 0.776302. These pooled minima include completed boundary trials and are descriptive selection results. The exporter independently enforces physical dense weights plus preprocessing; some horizon-specific bundles exceed their nominal cap and are refused rather than mislabelled compliant.
